# Source Research Dossier

Assessment date: 2026-08-17

## Course Research Request

- Name: Banking and Financial Services Foundations for AI Solution Teams
- Description: A practical introduction to banking operations, products, customer journeys, controls, technology, and regulation for JavaScript and Python developers, Pega architects, technical leads, and AI solution designers creating AI solutions for banks.
- Ordered topics:
  1. Banking ecosystem, business models, participants, balance sheets, money creation, and core operating functions
  2. B2C retail banking: onboarding, deposits, cards, payments, consumer lending, servicing, complaints, fraud, and customer experience
  3. B2B and SME banking: onboarding, accounts, payments, cash management, servicing, and relationship management
  4. Payments and transaction banking: authorisation, clearing, settlement, reconciliation, exceptions, cash management, and payment fraud
  5. Corporate lending: origination, credit assessment, underwriting, approval, documentation, collateral, servicing, monitoring, collections, and recoveries
  6. Trade finance: documentary credits, guarantees, documentary checks, sanctions exposure, and cross-border transactions
  7. Wealth management: client segmentation, suitability, advice, portfolio management, execution, custody, reporting, conflicts, and fiduciary duties
  8. Markets and investment banking: trading, securities, custody, settlement, market abuse, and conflicts of interest
  9. Treasury, liquidity, and capital: funding, liquidity management, interest-rate exposure, capital requirements, and financial resilience
  10. KYC and Client Lifecycle Management: identification, due diligence, risk rating, beneficial ownership, periodic review, and offboarding
  11. Financial and Economic Crime: AML/CFT, sanctions, fraud, transaction monitoring, investigations, alerts, and suspicious-activity reporting
  12. Risk and control model: credit, market, liquidity, operational, conduct, compliance, fraud, and model risk; front, middle, and back office; and the three lines model
  13. Data, core systems, finance, and regulatory reporting: ledgers, systems of record, data quality, lineage, reconciliation, recordkeeping, audit trails, and reporting
  14. Conduct and customer protection: Consumer Duty, complaints, vulnerability, hardship, fair treatment, accessibility, and remediation
  15. Open banking, open finance, embedded finance, Banking-as-a-Service, APIs, consent, and third-party ecosystems
  16. Operational resilience and cybersecurity: critical services, continuity, incidents, outsourcing, cloud, third parties, DORA, privacy, and security controls
  17. Responsible banking AI: suitable automation boundaries, human oversight, explainability, evidence, monitoring, escalation, AI regulation, and model risk
  18. AI opportunity assessment: connecting automation to CSAT, cost-to-serve, processing time, error rates, losses, control effectiveness, and customer outcomes
- Audience: JavaScript developers, Python developers, Pega architects, technical leads, and AI solution designers with limited banking-domain experience
- Level: Beginner
- Language: en-US for the Course and every Source
- Intended learning outcome: Given a banking AI use case, analyse its customer journey, operational process, actors, data, systems, risks, and regulatory controls, then propose an appropriate AI-assisted solution with human oversight and measurable CSAT, cost, speed, and control outcomes.
- Learning-time budget: 720 minutes (12 hours)
- Regulatory scope: International banking foundations with EU and UK examples; the Course is educational and is not legal or compliance advice.

## Recommendation

Recommended ordered selection: **C01, C02, C03, C04, C05, C06, C07, C08, C09, C10**.

The ten-Source path progresses from the banking and supervision foundation through payments, lending, wealth and markets operations, trade finance, financial crime, conduct, data and digital finance, and finally banking-specific AI governance. The combined Estimated Activity Duration is **700 minutes**, leaving **20 minutes** inside the approved **720-minute** Course budget.

The most important limitations are deliberate foundation-level boundaries: detailed accounting and ALM calculations, jurisdiction-specific insolvency and complaints procedures, quantitative trading and portfolio construction, ISO 20022 implementation, and vendor-specific core-banking architecture are not taught in depth. The selected path nevertheless introduces every approved topic and gives technical learners a control-aware vocabulary for analysing banking AI use cases.

This recommendation is advisory. No candidate becomes a selected Source until the Course Requester explicitly confirms an ordered selection.
