## Recommendation

Select **R01** as the direct replacement for C05. It best preserves the activity's beginner teaching purpose and 65-minute allocation. Its fundamentals remain relevant, but its 2007 publication date means current rules and jurisdiction-specific practices must be checked against FATF C06 and current institutional policy rather than treated as current legal guidance.

With R01, the complete ordered path remains **700 minutes**. With R02, it would be **685 minutes**.
